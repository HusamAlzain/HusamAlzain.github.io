# The future of work with intelligent agents

We are moving from simply using AI tools to working alongside autonomous agents.

## What is changing?

- **Autonomy**: Agents can make considered decisions.
- **Integration**: They work smoothly with existing systems.
- **Speed**: Complex tasks can be executed in seconds.

---

## Conclusion

Staying close to these technologies is the key to succeeding in the near future.
